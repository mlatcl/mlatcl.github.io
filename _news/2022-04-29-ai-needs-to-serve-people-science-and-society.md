On 29 April 2022 Neil Lawrence and [Jess Montgomery](/people/jessica-montgomery.html) published [*AI needs to serve people, science, and society: A vision for ai@cam*](https://www.cam.ac.uk/system/files/aicam_review_april22.pdf). The review followed surveys and workshops with Cambridge’s AI community through winter 2021 and spring 2022, with support from Anne Ferguson-Smith and Paulina Pieprzyca.

The document sets out ai@cam as a challenge-led University mission: connect Cambridge’s AI research across disciplines; build partnerships with practitioners and affected communities; create platforms for innovation and knowledge transfer; and keep equity and inclusion in the design of what gets built. It treats AI both as a research field and as an accelerator of work elsewhere, and warns that benefits and harms are unevenly shared unless universities help close digital divides.

A [University of Cambridge story](https://www.cam.ac.uk/stories/ai-at-cam) accompanied the launch. The [PDF of the review](https://www.cam.ac.uk/system/files/aicam_review_april22.pdf) remains the public record of that vision. The mission’s first public programme announcement came later, with the [AI-deas academic unicorns](/news/2024-02-05-aicam-announces-ai-deas-academic-unicorns.html).
