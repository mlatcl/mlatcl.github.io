On 2 August 2023 arXiv [introduced a new governance model](https://blog.arxiv.org/2023/08/02/introducing-arxivs-new-governance-model/). New bylaws took effect on 1 July 2023; the former Member and Scientific Advisory Boards were retired, and three councils were created — Editorial, Institutions, and Science.

Neil Lawrence is an inaugural member of the [Science Advisory Council](https://info.arxiv.org/about/people/science_advisory_council.html) for the 2023–2026 term. The SAC advises on intellectual impact and long-term strategy: mission, significant expansions, standards, new fields and sections, and how scholarly work is produced and shared across disciplines. It is distinct from the Editorial Advisory Council, which handles content-moderation policy.

The appointment reflects the lab’s stake in open research infrastructure that machine learning, like other fields, depends on.
