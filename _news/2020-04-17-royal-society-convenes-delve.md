On 17 April 2020 the Royal Society [announced DELVE](https://royalsociety.org/news/2020/04/royal-society-convenes-data-analytics-group-to-tackle-covid-19/) — Data Evaluation and Learning for Viral Epidemics — a multidisciplinary group to analyse emerging national and international data on COVID-19 responses and feed findings into SAGE.

Neil Lawrence served across the action team, working group and steering committee. [Jessica Montgomery](/people/jessica-montgomery.html), then a senior policy adviser at the Royal Society, was a key member of the DELVE secretariat from inception until her move to Cambridge in September 2020. [Andrei Paleyes](/people/andrei-paleyes.html) joined the action team, contributing later to modelling software and open datasets.

The group’s remit was to complement the UK’s strategic evidence base: compare measures across countries, use new data as it appeared, and coordinate with other research efforts. Outputs through 2020 are collected on the [DELVE site](https://rs-delve.github.io/) and summarised on the lab’s [DELVE project page](/projects/delve-data-evaluation-and-learning-for-viral-epidemics.html).
