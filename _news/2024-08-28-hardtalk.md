On 28 August 2024 Neil Lawrence appeared on BBC [*HARDtalk*](https://www.bbc.co.uk/programmes/m0022h8k) with Stephen Sackur. The episode is [*Neil Lawrence – Professor of Machine Learning, Cambridge University*](https://www.bbc.co.uk/programmes/m0022h8k); an audio version for World Service listeners is [*Neil Lawrence: Being human in the age of the machine*](https://www.bbc.com/audio/play/w3ct5t4h).

The interview centres on [*The Atomic Human*](/publications/the-atomic-human.html), published in the UK that June: what remains of human intelligence once machines take on tasks we once treated as uniquely ours, and whether people — not only specialists — will steer AI as a tool rather than become tools of it. Sackur presses the familiar divide between embrace and fear; Neil’s line is that the decisive factor is human judgment in development and deployment, not the technology alone.

Further context is on [Neil’s book page](https://inverseprobability.com/atomic-human/).
