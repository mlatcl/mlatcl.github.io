On 1 August 2022 ELLIS announced that its members had [elected a new Board](https://ellis.eu/news/ellis-members-elect-a-new-board-for-the-next-two-years) following the July general assembly. Neil Lawrence was among five newly elected Fellows joining the management body of the European Laboratory for Learning and Intelligent Systems.

ELLIS is a pan-European network of AI research excellence; the Board sets its strategic agenda. Neil’s election sits alongside the group’s work with European AI networks, including the [ELISE / European Network of AI Excellence Centres](/projects/european-network-of-ai-excellence-centres.html) programme. He remains listed on the [ELLIS Board](https://ellis.eu/about/board); ELLIS later profiled him in its [Meet the Board](https://ellis.eu/news/meet-the-board-neil-lawrence) series (January 2026).
