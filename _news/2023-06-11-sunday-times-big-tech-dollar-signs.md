On 11 June 2023 the *Sunday Times* published Neil Lawrence’s opinion piece [*Our lives may be enhanced by AI, but Big Tech just sees dollar signs*](https://www.thetimes.com/business-money/technology/article/our-lives-may-be-enhanced-by-ai-but-big-tech-just-sees-dollar-signs-2g5xcsk35).

The article contrasts what the public asks for from AI — healthcare, education, social care, security — with where much commercial investment has gone. Neil argues that grand narratives of artificial general intelligence suit incumbent interests, and that the harder task is deploying tools that re-empower the people who already hold society together. He also pushes back on existential “apex intelligence” framing associated with Geoffrey Hinton’s departure from Google: human intelligence was never the apex in every respect; machines already exceed us in many, and the real risk is who controls the infrastructure of information.

The piece sits with later policy and media work on [AI adoption](/projects/ai-adoption.html) and with [*The Atomic Human*](/publications/the-atomic-human.html).
