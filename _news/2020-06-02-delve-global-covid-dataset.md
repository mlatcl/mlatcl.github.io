On 2 June 2020 DELVE released its [Global COVID-19 Dataset](https://rs-delve.github.io/data_software/2020/06/02/global-dataset.html) — an openly licensed, research-ready consolidation of country-level data from multiple sources. Authors include Avishkar Bhoopchand, [Andrei Paleyes](/people/andrei-paleyes.html), Kevin Donkers, Nenad Tomasev and Ulrich Paquet.

The join covers cases, deaths, tests, non-pharmaceutical interventions, excess mortality, mobility, weather and other metadata for up to 170 countries, aimed at analysts comparing international responses. A daily-updated CSV and Python API live on the [covid19_datasets](https://github.com/rs-delve/covid19_datasets/) repository.

The release sits with the group’s [DELVE](/projects/delve-data-evaluation-and-learning-for-viral-epidemics.html) programme of open tools and reports during the first year of the pandemic.
