On 3 September 2024 PublicAffairs published the US and Canadian edition of Neil Lawrence’s book as [*The Atomic Human: What Makes Us Unique in the Age of AI*](https://www.hachettebookgroup.com/titles/neil-d-lawrence/the-atomic-human/9781541705128/). The UK edition, [*The Atomic Human: Understanding Ourselves in the Age of AI*](/news/2024-06-06-the-atomic-human.html), appeared with Allen Lane on 6 June 2024.

The North American release keeps the same core argument — that slicing away the facets of intelligence machines can take on leaves an indivisible human core — under a subtitle that stresses uniqueness in the age of AI. Publisher details and formats are on the [Hachette / PublicAffairs page](https://www.hachettebookgroup.com/titles/neil-d-lawrence/the-atomic-human/9781541705128/); further context is on [Neil’s book page](https://inverseprobability.com/atomic-human/).
