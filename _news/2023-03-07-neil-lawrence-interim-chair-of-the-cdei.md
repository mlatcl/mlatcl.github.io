Neil Lawrence, appointed a [member of the Centre for Data Ethics and Innovation advisory board](/news/2022-01-25-neil-lawrence-joins-the-cdei-advisory-board.html) in January 2022, later became its interim chair. He chaired the board at its meeting on 7 March 2023, the first meeting whose [minutes](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/board-meeting-agendas-and-minutes/centre-for-data-ethics-and-innovation-minutes-advisory-board-meeting-6) record him in that role.

The board held its last meeting on 5 September 2023 and [closed on 9 September 2023](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/advisory-board-of-the-centre-for-data-ethics-and-innovation/advisory-board-of-the-centre-for-data-ethics-and-innovation). Neil’s reflection on the work, as member and interim chair, is on the [Responsible Technology Adoption Unit blog](https://rtau.blog.gov.uk/2023/09/26/championing-responsible-innovation-reflections-from-the-cdei-advisory-board/).
