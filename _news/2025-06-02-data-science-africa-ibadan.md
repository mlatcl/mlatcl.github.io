[Data Science Africa](http://datascienceafrica.org/) held its 2025 summer school and workshop at the University of Ibadan, Nigeria, from 2–6 June 2025. The grass-roots meeting combines training in machine learning and data science with a research workshop on African challenges.

Neil Lawrence gave a keynote drawing on themes from *The Atomic Human*. [Oluwatomisin Dada](/people/oluwatomisin-dada.html) served as a programme chair for the meeting. An ICCS account of the visit is [here](https://iccs.cam.ac.uk/news/data-science-africa-2025-summer-school-and-workshop); the event archive is at [datascienceafrica.org](http://datascienceafrica.org/dsa-2025).
