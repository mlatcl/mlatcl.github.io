On 23 February 2023 the Competition and Markets Authority appointed Neil Lawrence to its group of independent digital experts advising the Digital Markets Unit. The nine experts were to advise on digital casework and on preparation for the new digital markets regime, for an initial two years. The announcement is on [GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/experts-appointed-as-uk-looks-to-level-digital-playing-field-for-consumers). The group has since been disbanded.
