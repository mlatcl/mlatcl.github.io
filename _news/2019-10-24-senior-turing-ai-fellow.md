On 24 October 2019 the Alan Turing Institute [announced the first five Turing AI Fellows](https://www.turing.ac.uk/news/welcoming-world-class-turing-ai-fellows-institute), appointed with the Office for AI and UKRI as part of the government’s AI skills and talent package. Neil Lawrence was named Senior Turing AI Fellow — the first such senior post — for a five-year programme beginning in December 2019.

The fellowship centres on machine learning systems design: the full pipeline from data acquisition to decision-making, with monitoring for performance, interpretability and fairness, and attention to the availability, quality and ethics of data. That agenda became the lab’s [AutoAI](/projects/autoai.html) programme — extending AutoML toward system-wide deployment, maintenance and monitoring.

Coverage of the wave of appointments includes [Diginomica](https://diginomica.com/first-wave-turing-ai-fellows-announced-keep-uk-forefront-ai-revolution). Neil’s own note on the fellowship is [here](https://inverseprobability.com/talks/notes/auto-ai.html).
