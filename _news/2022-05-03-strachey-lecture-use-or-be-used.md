On 3 May 2022 Neil Lawrence gave the [Strachey Lecture in Computing Science](https://www.cs.ox.ac.uk/seminars/strachey/) at the University of Oxford. The termly series is named after [Christopher Strachey](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christopher_Strachey), the first Professor of Computation at Oxford.

The lecture, *Use or be Used: Regaining Control of AI*, argued that machine intelligences deliver decisions derived from data at a scale far beyond natural counterparts — and that if we devolve decision-making to machines without understanding how they operate, we lose control over our destiny. Much of the debate around AI mistakes machine intelligence for a reflection of our own; the talk held that to control the machine we need to understand the machine, and to understand the machine we first need to understand ourselves.

The [lecture page](https://www.cs.ox.ac.uk/seminars/2466.html) is on the Oxford Computer Science site.
