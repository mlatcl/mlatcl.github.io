On 25 January 2022 the [Centre for Data Ethics and Innovation](/projects/centre-for-data-ethics-and-innovation.html) advisory board was told that Neil Lawrence had been appointed a member. Edwina Dunn was interim chair. The [minutes](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/board-meeting-agendas-and-minutes/centre-for-data-ethics-and-innovation-minutes-advisory-board-meeting-2) record the appointment.
