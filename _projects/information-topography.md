The **Information Topography** programme develops a generative theory of
communication structure. Shannon's information theory characterises what can be
transmitted through a channel taken as given. It does not explain where channels
come from, why particular participants are linked, or how those links change when
information conditions change.

Our premise is that communication structures are governed by constraints on
information dynamics. Combining information-conservation constraints with
maximum-entropy production yields a state-dependent flow geometry in which the
Fisher information metric acts as a conductance tensor — an *information
topography*. Because structure emerges from constrained dynamics, changes to the
dynamics predict changes in structure.

The programme builds on *The Atomic Human* (Lawrence, 2024), which framed human
intelligence through the embodiment factor — the extreme imbalance between human
computation and communication — and on the inaccessible-game formalism, which
derives topographic dynamics from information-theoretic axioms without
pre-specified agents or channels. Teaching in [L172 Information, Energy and
Intelligence](https://mlatcl.github.io/iei/) develops the same
mathematical machinery with students.

## Research themes

* Axiomatic foundations of information topography (inaccessible game; open-system
  extensions; classical and quantum representations of precision/capacity
  trade-offs)
* Fisher conductance as a predictive geometry for bottlenecks and reorganisation
* Information-theoretic accounts of regulation and judgement (Good Regulator
  conditions; action-sufficient representations)
* Links from constrained information dynamics to cybernetic viability and
  organisational communication structure

## Relationship to other programmes

Information Topography supplies the mathematical core of the broader InfoTop
agenda. Predictions and instruments are tested in engineered multi-agent systems
in the [Interfaces](/projects/interfaces.html) programme, and in institutions
adopting AI in the [AI Adoption](/projects/ai-adoption.html) programme.
