**aICU** is a clinically embedded research programme founded at Karolinska
Institutet and Stockholm South General Hospital (Södersjukhuset). It develops
and evaluates AI-based decision support for intensive care. Work spans
retrospective model development, external validation, human-centred design and
evaluation, and prospective clinical studies, supported by large-scale
longitudinal ICU data and close integration with the clinical environment in
Stockholm.

The programme’s first major clinical application is decision support around
mechanical ventilation and extubation, including dynamic prediction of
ventilation duration and extubation failure. The methods, infrastructure and
evaluation framework are designed to support research across other complex and
time-sensitive decisions in intensive care.

## Cambridge partnership

International collaboration began with the University of Cambridge. ML@CL works
with the aICU team on modelling, systems, and human–computer interaction for
clinical decision support — including early clinical integration and co-design,
and a systems approach to an ecosystem of models that support decision-making
rather than standalone tools. On the Karolinska programme page, Cambridge
members listed include Christian Cabrera, Diana Robinson, Radzim Sendyka and
Carl Henrik Ek.

On the Cambridge side the work connects to the [Interfaces](/projects/interfaces.html)
programme and is supported by the [Accelerate Programme for Scientific
Discovery](https://science.ai.cam.ac.uk/).

Further details are on the [Karolinska Institutet aICU programme
page](https://ki.se/en/research/research-areas-centres-and-networks/research-groups/anaesthesia-and-intensive-care-rebecka-rubenson-wahlinanna-schandls-research-group/the-aicu-project-developing-next-generation-decision-support-tools-for-intensive-care).
