The [GDS Responsible AI Advisory
Panel](https://www.gov.uk/government/groups/gds-responsible-ai-advisory-panel)
was established by the Government Digital Service (GDS) under the Department for
Science, Innovation and Technology (DSIT). It brings together views from across
the public sector, industry, academia and civil society on the responsible use
of AI, as part of the government’s AI for the public good agenda set out in the
Roadmap for Modern Digital Government.

The panel provides expert strategic advice to DSIT ministers and senior
officials, informs GDS on best practice for developing responsible AI, and
offers constructive challenge on government’s approach to responsible AI
products. It is chaired by Jeni Tennison and held its first meeting on 2 March
2026.

Neil is a panel member, contributing expertise on machine learning, data
governance and AI systems.
