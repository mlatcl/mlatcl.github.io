title: Creating a Pathway to Successful Real-World Data Trusts
abstract: Moving data trusts from theory into practice requires attention to the
  institutional conditions that allow new forms of data stewardship to operate
  successfully. Drawing on research funded by the Data Trusts Initiative, this
  article identifies an emerging framework for operationalising data trusts
  across community dialogue, technical systems, legal mechanisms, business
  models and use cases. It argues for further practical experimentation through
  case studies, pilot projects and testbeds capable of resolving questions that
  can only be answered through implementation.
