abstract: |
  In this paper we introduce the inaccessible game, an information-theoretic
  dynamical system constructed from four axioms. The first three axioms are known
  and define information loss in the system. The fourth is a novel information
  isolation axiom that assumes our system is isolated from observation, making
  it observer-independent and exchangeable. Under this isolation axiom, total
  marginal entropy is conserved: ∑ᵢ hᵢ = C. We consider maximum entropy
  production in the game and show that the dynamics exhibit a GENERIC-like
  structure combining reversible and irreversible components.
