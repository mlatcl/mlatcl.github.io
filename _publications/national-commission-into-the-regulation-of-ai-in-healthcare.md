title: "National Commission into the Regulation of AI in Healthcare: Recommendations for a future regulatory framework"
abstract: |
  The National Commission into the Regulation of AI in Healthcare was
  established by the MHRA in September 2025 as an independent expert
  advisory body. It was asked to consider how the UK’s regulatory and
  assurance framework should evolve to support the safe, effective and
  responsible use of AI in healthcare. This report sets out the
  Commission’s recommendations for a future regulatory framework. Its
  central conclusion is that regulation and assurance of AI in
  healthcare must become more proportionate, lifecycle-based and
  system-wide.
