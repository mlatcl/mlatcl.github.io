The [Fourth UK AI Conference](http://uk-ai.org/ukai2026/) runs on 29–30 September 2026 at the Hilton Nottingham. This year’s meeting is supported by the Turing AI Fellowship *Somabotics: Creatively Embodying AI*, led from the University of Nottingham. Steve Benford is local chair; Neil Lawrence chairs the conference. [Christian Cabrera](/people/christian-cabrera.html) serves on the programme committee.

As with earlier editions, the meeting combines plenary talks with posters and short talks across AI theory, systems, applications and responsible AI. An evening screening, *AI LENS: Stories from the Latent Space*, is planned for 29 September at Broadway Cinema. Registration is via the University of Nottingham; details are on the [conference site](http://uk-ai.org/ukai2026/).

See the [community building](/projects/community-building.html) project for the wider programme of meetings.
