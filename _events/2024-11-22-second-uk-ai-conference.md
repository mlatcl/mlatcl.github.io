The [Second UK AI Conference](http://uk-ai.org/ukai2024/) took place on 22 November 2024 at the Exchange, University of Birmingham. The meeting continued the series started in 2023, with plenary talks, short talks and posters from researchers across the UK.

[Christian Cabrera](/people/christian-cabrera.html) was the principal organising contact and a volume editor for the conference proceedings, published as [PMLR volume 295](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v295/). Neil Lawrence remains on the series committee and is a series editor for the [Proceedings of Machine Learning Research](https://proceedings.mlr.press/).

The conference is part of the lab’s [community building](/projects/community-building.html) work.
