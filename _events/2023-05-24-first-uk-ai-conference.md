The [First UK AI Conference](http://uk-ai.org/ukai2023/) ran on 24–25 May 2023 at the Natural History Museum in London. Organised by and for the Turing AI Fellows, it was the first national conference in what has become the [UK AI Conference](https://uk-ai.org/) series.

Neil Lawrence co-chaired the meeting with Per Kristian Lehre, Maria Liakata and Anna Scaife. [Christian Cabrera](/people/christian-cabrera.html) served on the programme committee. The event brought research groups from across the UK together for talks and posters on AI theory, methods and applications.

The series continues under the [community building](/projects/community-building.html) programme, with later editions organised principally by Christian.
