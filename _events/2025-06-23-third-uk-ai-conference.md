The [Third UK AI Conference](http://uk-ai.org/ukai2025/) ran on 23–24 June 2025 at the Gibbs Building in London, with support from HDR UK. As with the Birmingham meeting, [Christian Cabrera](/people/christian-cabrera.html) was the principal organising contact and a member of the programme committee. Neil Lawrence remains on the series committee.

The series connects AI researchers nationwide for talks and posters across theory, systems, applications and responsible AI. A [fourth conference](http://uk-ai.org/ukai2026/) is scheduled for 29–30 September 2026 in Nottingham.

See the [community building](/projects/community-building.html) project for the wider programme of meetings.
