ML@CL held research days on 8–9 June 2026 to share current work across the group and with visiting colleagues from [Trent AI](https://trent.ai/). [Christian Cabrera](/people/christian-cabrera.html) organised the programme; [Hanni Sondermann](/people/hanni-sondermann.html) handled logistics, including accommodation at Queens’ for visitors.

**Monday 8 June** — Queens’ College (Room DD46), afternoon talks:

- Zhenwen Dai (Trent AI)
- [Christian Cabrera](/people/christian-cabrera.html)
- [Joery de Vries](/people/joery-de-vries.html)
- [Harry Allen](/people/harry-allen.html)
- [Raphael Trumpp](/people/raphael-trumpp.html)
- [Ilai Shraga](/people/ilai-shraga.html)

The group then walked to [The Green Man](https://www.thegrantchestergreenman.com/) in Grantchester for dinner.

**Tuesday 9 June** — Pembroke College (Room 3, 4 Mill Lane), morning talks:

- [Radzim Sendyka](/people/radzim-sendyka.html)
- [Carl Henrik Ek](/people/carl-henrik-ek.html)
- [Erik Englesson](/people/erik-englesson.html)
- [Neil Lawrence](/people/neil-d-lawrence.html)

Lunch was informal; the afternoon was left open for collaboration and other activities.
