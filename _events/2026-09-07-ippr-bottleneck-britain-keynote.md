On 7 September Neil Lawrence gave a keynote at the Financial Times for the launch of the IPPR paper [*Bottleneck Britain: How digital competition could power UK growth and sovereignty*](https://www.ippr.org/articles/bottleneck-britain).

The event, [Time to Reset: Unlocking Growth and Sovereignty Through Digital Competition](https://www.eventbrite.co.uk/e/time-to-reset-unlocking-growth-sovereignty-through-digital-competition-tickets-1996092372018), asked how the Competition and Markets Authority’s Digital Markets Unit can use the digital competition regime to support growth across the UK and strengthen tech sovereignty. Neil had advised that unit from 2023 as a member of its [independent digital expert group](/news/2023-02-23-digital-markets-unit-expert-group.html), which has since been disbanded.
