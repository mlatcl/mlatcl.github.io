Andrei is a visiting researcher with Professor Neil Lawrence's group. He completed his PhD at ML@CL in 2024 and is now Chief Engineer at Pasteur Labs. His primary research focus is the intersection of software systems and machine learning. He also sometimes works on Bayesian optimisation and its applications. Prior to joining ML@CL he received a Masters degree from Belarusian State University, and then spent a number of years as a software engineer, working on everything from web applications to data center networks.
