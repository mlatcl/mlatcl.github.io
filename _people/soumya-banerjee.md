  Soumya is a Senior Lecturer in Computer Science at the University of York,
  working on explainable and trustworthy AI with applications in healthcare and
  computational biology. He was previously an Assistant Research Professor on
  the Accelerate Programme at Cambridge. He analyses complex problems and
  implements statistical and machine learning techniques for deriving insights
  from large amounts of data, working closely with experimentalists and
  clinicians.

  Before Cambridge he worked in industry and held research positions at Oxford,
  CSIRO, Harvard Medical School and the Max Planck Institute. He collaborates
  with domain experts in finance, healthcare, immunology, virology and cell
  biology, and has worked with clinicians and patients on patient and public
  involvement to build trust in AI algorithms.
